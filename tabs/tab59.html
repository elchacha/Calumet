★ Key features

  • Every relationship field of the org in one page: lookup, master-detail, hierarchy and junction.
  • Pick an object, then split its relations into Parents (its own fields) and Children (fields of other objects pointing at it).
  • Filter by type, by field label or API name, and by referenced object; hide managed-package objects.
  • Sort the object list by label or by number of relationships; sort the table by object, field, type or references.
  • See nillable, cascade or restricted delete and roll-up fields at a glance.
  • Results are cached per org; Refresh re-reads them and Force refresh bypasses the describe cache.
