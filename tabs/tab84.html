★ Key features

  • Groups conflicts into field families — 268 findings become 24 decisions.
  • Four conflict kinds, each failing differently: same-field writers, order-dependent, read-after-write, async.
  • Per-object execution timeline showing the phase each automation runs in.
  • AI reading of a family as a step chain, where every step cites its source automation.
  • Runs offline from the dependency graph cache; the org is only queried on Refresh.
