How to use it

  • Pick two pages. The first line is the main org of Calumet. Choose Org 2, then the FlexiPage to compare with, typically the same page in a sandbox and in production.
  • Compare reads the two page definitions and matches components one by one. Calumet reuses the XML it already downloaded (the date is shown next to each page); tick ignore cache (force retrieve) to download both pages again.
  • Compare VSCode opens both XML files in VSCode's diff, for a line-by-line reading.

The four views

  • Résumé: every change, grouped as added, deleted, modified or moved, with the component, its type and its region.
  • Vue Diff: one card per component. Filter by kind of change, click a card to see its properties before and after.
  • Vue Mise en page: version 1 on the left, version 2 on the right, region by region. Dashed slots keep the rows aligned when a component exists on one side only.
  • Mise en page (beta): a rough merged sketch of the page. The real template geometry is unknown, so the columns are indicative.

Reading the colours

  • Green added, red deleted, orange modified, purple moved, grey unchanged. The five tiles at the top are buttons: click one to keep only that kind of change.
  • A moved component has not changed, it only sits at another position or in another region. Look at the orange ones first.
