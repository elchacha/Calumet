★ Key features

  • Usage is the number of records that populate the field; Percent is its share of the object's records.
  • LastModifyDate is an age pill: red under 90 days, orange under 1 year, amber 1 to 3 years, green beyond 3 years (dormant).
  • Picklist fields authorised by a record type are left out as false positives.
  • The filter box accepts several terms separated by semicolons. Export to json saves the current view together with its filters.
