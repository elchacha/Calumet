Double-click a row to open the WebLink in Salesforce Setup. Dates show as relative ages; hover a date for the full value. Hover a badge for details; click "On layout" to list the hosting layouts.

★ Key features

  • Extract scans custom buttons and custom links and cross-checks them against the buttons referenced by every Page Layout.
  • Type badges: JavaScript and sControl links are deprecated by Salesforce and are priority cleanup candidates.
  • Display badge tells whether the item is a Button, a Link or a Mass action.
  • Rows are sorted by oldest Last Modified first, the strongest cleanup candidates.
  • Multi-keyword filter with AND (use ;) and a live count of shown versus total links.
  • Double-click a row to open the WebLink in Salesforce Setup.
  • Detection does not cover Apex, Flow or LWC references: always confirm before deleting.
