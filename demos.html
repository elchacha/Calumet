Interactive demos

Walk through Calumet screen by screen. Everything runs in your browser: nothing to install, no Salesforce org to connect, and none of it is real data.

🗄️
DataSeeding — copy data from one org to another
Pick a few seed records and let Calumet follow their relations: parents, children, import order, id remapping. See how failed queries and rejected records are reported, and how to retry only those.
15 steps · about 5 minutes
→ 📦
Deployment pipeline — ChangeTracker to Deploy
Track what changed, retrieve it, control it, deploy it, and keep a history of every deployment.
Guided walkthrough
→ 🛡️
User Access Score — who holds the most power
Rank every user by the privileges of their profile and permission sets. Filter by level or dormant accounts, choose a weighting, see where a score comes from, compare users and spot permission sets that add nothing.
8 steps · about 4 minutes
→ 🧩
FlexiPage Diff — compare two Lightning pages
Pick the same page in two orgs, compare them, and read what was added, deleted, modified or moved: as a summary, as cards with before and after, and side by side region by region.
7 steps · about 3 minutes
→ 🔑
Permission Sets — who can do what
Browse every permission set and group, search by name, content or user, see what changed and who got what, compare overlaps, and open the detail of any set.
6 steps · about 4 minutes
→
